# Etiology
## Bell's Palsy 
> Bell's Palsy = (Acute idiopathic peripheral facial palsy)

懷疑Bell's Palsy最重要的ddx是 排除：1. stroke 2. ==Ear infection==！
因為耳朵＆mastoid感染有可能會影響CN7

# Treatment
### Tx of Bell's Palsy
- 口服類固醇
	- Prednisone 60 mg PO daily for 5 days, followed by a 5-day taper
	- Start early (i.e., within 48–72 hours of symptom onset)

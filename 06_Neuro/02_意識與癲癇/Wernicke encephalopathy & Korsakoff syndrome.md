# 症狀
最重要的三個症狀：
1. **abnormal mental status** 
2. **ataxia** 
3. **eye movement abnormalities**
	1. gaze induced nystagmus
	2. Ophthalmoplegia
# Management
give thiamine early, and don’t delay dextrose to give thiamine first
> [!clinical] ER Tx
> - 如果懷疑，在任何檢查之前就要**迅速給予 high dose Thiamine** !!
> - Glucose 一定要*在 thiamine 後面給*！（因為糖會增加thiamine demand和惡化encephalopathy）
